About Encoded Radiance Studio

Founder Profile

Sarai Hannah Ajai

Founder and Creative Director
Encoded Radiance Studio

Encoded Radiance Studio was founded by Sarai Hannah Ajai and operates under her creative direction. The studio develops original digital media, audio productions, visual systems, motion concepts, interactive web experiences, and technology-directed creative projects through documented production workflows.

Sarai Hannah Ajai directs concept development, creative planning, authorship review, source-integrity verification, production standards, editorial review, and final release approval. The studio’s visual language combines structured signal systems, light, sound, geometry, motion, and expansive digital space.

Founder Direction

Sarai Hannah Ajai directs the studio’s creative decisions, project scope, production priorities, review standards, and final publication approval.

Official Attribution

Encoded Radiance Studio is officially identified as founded and creatively directed by Sarai Hannah Ajai.

Studio projects proceed through defined stages of research, concept planning, development, source review, refinement, documentation, and release preparation.

The studio develops distinctive digital work that combines technical precision, documented authorship, purposeful design, and expressive presentation.
